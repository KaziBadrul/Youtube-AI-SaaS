# Private Alpha invitation validation

Frozen Private Alpha baseline v1 — 2026-10-05. Architecture Freeze reconciles accepted decisions; it authorizes neither implementation nor capability activation.

This is the future real-project invitation gate, not a reopening of S1–S9. Current owner ceiling is $40 USD per Asia/Dhaka calendar month. Ten real English landscape topic/pasted-script projects span 3–10 minutes; financial admission/funding must be independently resolved for each. A conservative ten-minute request may be blocked under the current envelope; product-range quality validation never bypasses that ceiling.

## Quality evidence

Apply the owner-accepted 2026-10-05 fidelity amendment in the Alpha specification. Distinguish ASR discrepancies, explicitly accepted meaning-preserving spoken variations, unresolved review findings and material failures. Record version-bound approval and verify captions/mappings account for accepted actual speech with independently valid boundaries. Retain successful work; do not treat a quality finding as an application crash or authorize automatic paid repair.

Evaluate ten real English 16:9 projects spanning topic and pasted-script inputs and the 3–10-minute primary range. At least eight must become publishable within 15 minutes of active scene-level correction each. Record defects and correction time for every project.

| Record for each project | Evidence to capture |
| --- | --- |
| Input and scope | Input type, narration language, estimated/actual duration and enabled features |
| Technical completion | Playable 1080p/30 fps MP4, complete narration, decodable images, correct scene order, no unintended blank gaps and readable enabled captions |
| Quality | Serious factual errors, caption readability, pacing, narration/audio agreement, intelligibility/pronunciation/glitches/pauses and narration/visual agreement |
| Correction | Active minutes, defects corrected, selective operations performed and final creator judgment |
| Cost and storage | Actual spend including paid failures/retries, successful-asset allowance usage and stored asset sizes |

Disabled captions are not a defect. Private non-blocking factual warnings do not make serious factual errors acceptable. Minor aesthetic preferences and subjective delivery are acceptable within the correction budget.

## Reliability and access evidence

Before invitations, demonstrate interrupted-generation resumption, independent failed-image retry, and preservation of unaffected assets after edits. Verify private identities and isolation: one tester cannot access another tester's projects/files. Verify predefined allowances are enforced.

Render behavior must follow the agreed contract: editing disabled during final render, cancellation/retry available, edit locks released/recovered, existing generated assets reused and the last successful export preserved.

## Non-English availability

Spanish, French, Bangla and Hindi are experimental. Before enabling each, verify narration generation, correct caption display and export. These checks do not extend the English publishability claim.

## Measured invitation limits

Do not invent fixed allowances or storage caps before measurements. Record concrete limits before invitations, based on actual production cost and asset sizes. The shared US$40/month ceiling includes generation, research, hosting, storage, tester usage and paid failures. Only explicit owner approval may increase this strict Alpha ceiling. Architecture must not assume it remains permanent after validation.

Provider cost bounds, deployment/provider choices, detailed per-asset validity checks and upload limits require a later operational specification. S9 V14 already passed conditional planning feasibility; live activation remains gated. The invitation gate has not been demonstrated by the spikes.

## Follow-up milestones

Standalone one-minute vertical output and Emphasis Text follow the core workflow. Their relative order is undecided; they are not requirements for the first end-to-end Alpha release. When Emphasis Text is enabled, its unresolved outdated selections/timings must be reviewed before export, fonts must support the selected language and placement must avoid caption collisions.
