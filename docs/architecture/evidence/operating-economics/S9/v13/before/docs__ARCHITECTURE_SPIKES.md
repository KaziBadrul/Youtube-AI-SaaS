## Dependencies and stopping rules

S4 must establish an authorized finite request bound before paid S5 or paid portions of S9. S6 needs a compatible media runtime; the installed build is not proof. S2/S3/S7/S8 must pass before invited users rely on execution/recovery. S9 must cover fixed infrastructure and generation together before choosing hosting and numerical invitation limits.

These are evidence dependencies, not a task sequence for implementation. Owner testing and invitations still follow the product validation plan; passing architecture fixtures does not establish eight-of-ten publishability.

A failed spike requires reconsideration of the affected recommendation or an explicit budget/scope decision. Do not conceal failure by silently adding services, bypassing controls, changing product behavior or increasing the ceiling. Vertical video and Emphasis Text are follow-up Alpha milestones and need their own capability evidence later; they do not block the first end-to-end release.
