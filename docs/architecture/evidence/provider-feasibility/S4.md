## Unknown outcomes and privacy

Preserve bytes and accounting once received; never regenerate solely because local validation/install failed. An expired lease does not prove a request ended. Submission/read timeout with no durable response ID: no documented idempotent resend or lookup-by-local-attempt guarantee. Retain liability and execution exclusion; owner investigates logs/billing/support. Only reconciliation evidence or explicit owner authorization accepting duplicate-charge risk permits a potentially overlapping billable replacement. Cancellation intent alone releases neither hold nor lane.

GenerateContent has no equivalent documented asynchronous get-result operation keyed by its response ID. Paid AI Studio logging can expose stored prompts/responses and aid manual matching; default GenerateContent storage is off, Interactions storage on. Logs need account configuration and are not guaranteed unique attempt matching, a full audit trail of failed submissions, or proof an absent request was never received. Stored Interactions use 55-day paid/1-day free defaults, paid settings offer 7/14/28/55 days and ID-based deletion; `store=false` removes retrieval/background benefits. **Do not enable retention silently**: align provider-side copies/logging/deletion with accepted privacy/deletion obligations, or keep them off and accept owner-only unknown resolution. No managed dataset retention is authorized.
