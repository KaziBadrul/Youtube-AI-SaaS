## Deterministic financial boundary decision

**Decision:** Use append-only financial events plus transactionally maintained reservations and balances. Separate owner operating budget, tester successful-output allowance and per-request authority. Admit jobs only when reservations fit; recheck and commit a concrete attempt reservation before every paid submission. Model outputs never create or extend authority.

**Requirements:** Strict configurable Alpha ceiling, successful-operation charging, paid failures, retries, transparent plans and crash-safe reconciliation.

**Alternatives:** Deduct only after generation; treat estimates as actual cost; one mutable balance mixing all liabilities; provider budget alerts as the sole control.

**Tradeoffs:** Conservative holds can block work while costs are uncertain. This is preferable to hiding exposure or repeating unknown paid calls. Application control can only guarantee a ceiling if provider request/billing bounds are trustworthy; budget alerts alone are not proof of a hard stop.

**Reversibility:** Allocation/display policy can change prospectively. Preserve immutable historical events and use correction entries, not silent history rewrites.

**Evidence needed:** Maximum request charges, SDK transport behavior, billed failure/timeout behavior and actual reconciliation sources. No provider/cost rate has been approved or verified.

## Distinct concepts

| Concept | Authority and meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Estimate | Expected usage under a versioned rate/configuration basis, with uncertainty. Not a spending grant or maximum guarantee. |
| Request authorization | Creator approval of scope, relevant inputs, selected models/configuration and maximum authorized cost. Create Video grants this authority within the system-enforced ceiling; displaying or reviewing the ceiling is optional. |
| Shared operating budget | Owner-configured period ceiling, currently US$40/month. Includes generation, research, hosting, storage, backup, rendering, tester usage and paid failures. Explicit owner approval can revise it; never hardcode it as permanent. |
| Shared reservation | Held expected maximum liability for authorized future/submitted work or known infrastructure charges; not spent cash and not free capacity. |
| Tester allowance | Free USD-equivalent successful-output usage allocation for a private identity. Exact amounts remain measurement-dependent. |
| Allowance reservation | Held maximum possible successful-output usage for admitted work. Failed outputs release this hold, even if the owner incurred cost. |
| Consumed allowance | Cost attribution for valid delivered paid outputs, including text/research/checking/images/narration and valid history results. Rendering/local transforms do not consume it. |
| Actual operating spend | Incurred external/infrastructure cost including failures. A timeout does not prove zero cost. Record observed/estimated-liability/settled certainty separately. |
| Reconciliation | Evidence-backed transition from held exposure to known actual/allowance events and released surplus. Authorized operator action, never LLM inference. |

Use integer USD subunits for invariants, with exact conversion calculations and conservative rounding at liability boundaries. Store original invoice currency/amount, rate/tax/fee basis and observation timestamps where relevant. Do not use binary floating-point or SQLite decimal behavior for admission. The displayed denomination is not a public credit exchange rate.

## Plan and admission

1. Prepare a cost-free bounded plan from input/configuration and known provider limits. Expensive research/script planning requires authorization too; do not run it secretly to discover the price of generation.
2. Compute the bounded estimate, maximum cost and execution scope internally. Optional Details may present them; no separate review is required. Record the actor, time and exact source signatures when Create Video authorizes the request.
3. In one short database transaction, validate ownership/current inputs and reserve required shared budget and tester allowance before admitting the request. If it cannot fit, persist a visible blocked/queued request without pretending it has reservations or owns the production lane.
4. Claim the production lane separately when eligible. Revalidate inputs, limits, cancellation and reservation immediately before each concrete attempt.
5. Before outbound submission, persist its attempt identity and maximum charge allocation. Commit before calling the provider; no database transaction spans the network call.

The worker may allocate concrete steps within an approved plan only if their validated scope/configuration and total bounds remain authorized. New scenes from a model response are not spending instructions. Unknown scene count/cost requires a safe bounded policy or pause for a revised plan; an attractive low estimate is not sufficient.

For each budget period, free capacity is the configured ceiling minus settled incurred spend, unreconciled maximum liabilities, reserved future work and reserved known infrastructure obligations. Do not double-count a liability both as settled spend and a still-live hold. Tester free capacity is allocated allowance minus consumed usage and active successful-output holds. Request capacity separately accounts for all issued/submitted liability and known charges under that request's maximum.

All admission/allocation tests and reservation events occur atomically with idempotency constraints. Repeated clicks/recovery cannot reserve or charge the same attempt twice. Arithmetic uses conservative bounds, not LLM-generated numbers or UI balances.

## Completion and failed attempts

| Outcome | Actual spend / shared liability | Tester allowance | Output behavior |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Valid output, exact cost known | Record incurred cost, convert corresponding hold once, release surplus | Consume valid delivered usage once, release surplus | Register version; select only if current inputs/intent match |
| Valid output, cost not yet authoritative | Retain maximum exposure or settled estimate with explicit uncertainty until reconciliation; no fictitious zero | Keep successful-output hold pending final attribution rather than charging an invented exact amount | Output may be delivered with pending usage clearly shown |
| Known failed/invalid output | Record charged failure if known; retain conservative exposure if its price is uncertain | Release successful-output hold | Previous compatible assets remain; expose failed attempt |
| Outcome/submission unknown | Retain maximum liability and applicable allowance hold; no automatic paid retry | Remains reserved until success/failure/delivery outcome established | Recovery/reconciliation required |
| Provider success, local persistence failure | Preserve/retrieve response and paid attempt identity | Charge only once valid output is registered/delivered | Retry persistence, not generation; reconcile crash window |
| Valid late result after edit/cancel | Record actual attempt cost | Consume delivered historical output usage once | History only, never automatic current selection |
| Render/local transformation | Record measured/attributable operating costs | No generation allowance deduction | Local retries reuse assets |

Unknown costs are never released solely because a client canceled, disconnected, a worker lease expired or a month ended. A reservation may be released only when the relevant unissued work is canceled or evidence bounds/settles its liability. Reconciliation uses provider usage/billing data or documented conservative settlement with operator evidence. If authoritative cost is unavailable, retain the bound and disclose uncertainty; do not claim precise actual charges.

## Retries

Automatic retry is at most two retries per failed operation, persisted across restarts. Each paid attempt is individually visible and authorized. Its maximum must fit remaining request authority, shared budget and allowance exposure. Failed attempts may reduce shared/request capacity even though they release tester successful-output holds. A valid delivered output after a retry consumes its delivered usage; previous failed charges remain actual owner spend.

Disable hidden SDK retries or account for them as concrete billable attempts before enabling the adapter. Known zero-submission failures can retry under the documented classifier; an ambiguous timeout is not one. A manual retry creates explicit renewed authority for remaining work; it cannot erase previous spend or claim earlier successful assets need regeneration.

## Month boundary and budget revisions

Use Asia/Dhaka calendar months. Attribute each attempt to its committed accounting period while retaining its external billing identity/timing. At the boundary, old unknown liabilities remain reserved in their original history; future attempts require reservation/admission in the new period while still respecting the existing request's total authority. Actual invoices can require correction entries; invoice arrival is not permission to ignore incurred prior liabilities.

Reserve known recurring hosting/storage/backup obligations, tax/fees and approved conversion margins before allocating generation. A refreshed period does not cancel ongoing recurring services or make their cost zero. Provider/host billing timing must be checked before promising the total ceiling; costs spanning periods need explicit reconciliation rather than silently resetting exposure.

Only the owner changes the ceiling through an auditable explicit decision. “Not fixed” means configurable, not unlimited. Increasing it affects eligibility; lowering it does not reverse existing charges. If committed liabilities exceed a revised cap, show the condition and block further paid submissions; do not silently drop liabilities or revoke already submitted calls. Exact allowance distribution/reserves are selected only after measurements.

## Failure, restoration and limits of enforcement

Database unavailable: launch no new paid calls. Disk persistence failed: preserve provider outcome/identity and reconcile before generation retry. Restored backup: pause paid work until real provider/infrastructure spending and deletion records are reconciled; a 24-hour project-data recovery gap must not recreate spending capacity that no longer exists.

Maximum cost requires documented finite billable dimensions: input/output limits, number/size of generated images, reference/input charges, search grounding, SDK retries and any other charged capability. Pin the rate basis and invalidate obsolete quotes/configurations before invocation. A capability without a defensible maximum is not eligible for unattended paid execution under this architecture. A free-tier label is not evidence of zero billing.

If actual charges exceed the verified bound, halt further paid admission and surface an owner incident; already spent money cannot be undone. The feasibility gate must establish why this should not occur, including infrastructure behavior. No claim that the current legacy Gemini calls satisfy this boundary is made.

Public pricing, paid plans, subscriptions, BYOK and marketplace economics are outside this model and remain deferred.
